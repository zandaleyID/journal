This journal started with a collage theme because collage works the way multimodal design does, with its separate pieces, each in its own mode, arranged until together they make meaning. For my final entry, I wanted to hand that arranging over to the reader, as opposed to summarising eight entries in one more block of text.

Each note below holds one idea I took from an earlier entry. Drag or click a note onto the board and it becomes part of one final image. You can place them in any order.
