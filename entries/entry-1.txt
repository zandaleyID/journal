transcript: Hi, I’m Zan. Welcome to my multimodal journal, which I created for my postgraduate diploma in instructional design. It follows how my thinking changed as I worked with theories of multimedia learning, universal design and instructional design frameworks over the last year. I’ve become really interested in the creative side of this field. I consider myself a creative person, and instructional design turned out to be far more creative than it first appeared to me. Beyond the important theory that underpins instructional design, creativity and empathy also shape every decision about how meaning gets communicated. And multimodality is where I see that most clearly. Each mode has its own strengths, and learners read them differently, which makes human-centred design so important, more than just following a simple formula. There’s a reason for the collage theme you’ll see throughout this journal. I think a collage works the same way as multimodal instructional design. It’s made of separate pieces, arranged and rearranged until together they tell one story. It’s layered, developing and never fully resolved, and nothing here is meant to be a final thought either. It’s simply me working through my own design identity, the same way I’d want any learner to have room to work through theirs. Thanks for joining me, and I hope you enjoy exploring. **On screen:** Zan speaks to camera in a pink checked shirt against a crumpled cream-paper collage background with a flower, a butterfly and a ladybird. Collage cut-outs appear on the left as she talks: her name in ransom-note letters, a brain with a plant, stickers labelled CTML + CATLM, UDL, Bloom’s, TPACK and SAMR, statues, a lightbulb, a hand holding a heart, an ear, eyes and an open mouth, and a butterfly.
