This frustration is what Moreno's Cognitive-Affective Theory of Learning with Media (CATLM) adds to the CTML critique I did for Assignment 1. Where CTML explains how we process multimedia information through dual channels, CATLM argues that motivation, emotion, and individual learner differences mediate how effectively that processing happens (Moreno, 2006). Looking back at the Coursera AI course I critiqued, when the voice recognition tool malfunctioned, my motivation to keep engaging with the AI dialogue dropped because the experience with the tool itself was frustrating. In CATLM terms, the tool's failure to recognise my voice was a barrier to engagement, not to understanding, which shows that a feature can fail a learner without the content being at fault (Moreno, 2006).

The course's Critical Thinking Framework (CTF) presented the reverse problem: media missing rather than media distracting. It's a five-step process, but it was only ever explained through text and narrated video. Because it's sequential and spatial, a diagram with a worked example would have conveyed it more efficiently (Mayer, 2024). I finished the course unclear on what the CTF looked like in reality, and I suspect that gap carried into my confusion during the PPE later on. ## Final thoughts
The voice tool showed me that a frustrating experience can drain motivation even when the content is manageable, which is what CATLM adds to CTML. The AI course's CTF presented a sequential process through text alone, when a diagram would have made its structure clearer. In both instances, the way the media was designed affected how effectively learning could take place.
