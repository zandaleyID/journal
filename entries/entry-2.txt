For my first task, a tool demonstration, I explored [Wakelet](https://wakelet.com/) (2014), a free curation tool that lets you gather links, images, videos, and notes into a collection and put them in a sequence. It definitely changed how I think about choosing tools for multimodal design. As a novice instructional designer, I'm still navigating the plethora of tools out there, so currently, orchestrating content feels more manageable than creating it from scratch.

## Final thoughts
Wakelet pushes one to think about sequencing: how to order and structure resources, guided by learning theories and ID models, so that a learner can move through them in a meaningful way. That's a skill I want to build, both professionally and personally. Its value lies in how thoughtfully you combine and sequence modes and resources, not in the fact that it can hold them all.
